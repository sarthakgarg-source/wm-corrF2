## Reading choices (confirm or correct)
1. "Signal" = the desk's weekly supertrend state (BUY/SELL). Correlation is measured inside the joint states of a pair.
2. "Current correlation" = 13-week rolling (one quarter). Chosen to move with the market; 26 or 52 weeks would be smoother and slower (`config.CORR_WINDOW`, `HIST_WINDOW`).
3. "Breaks the correlation" = an index's 13-week correlation with the others falling 1 sd below its own 2-year norm (`CB_Z`, `CB_NORM`); ties by date are resolved with depth-at-break and size-of-drop. "Breaks in price" is read three ways — price peak, supertrend SELL flip, −10% cross — and reported separately, because they need not agree.
4. A sell-off = 10% fall in N100 (`DD_THRESHOLD`); the break line for each asset is 10% from its own high (`DD_CROSS`).
5. Price indices, not TRI.
6. "Deviation" = the z-score of the pair's log price ratio against its own trailing 52 weeks (`PAIR_WINDOW`), the same measure as wm-VIXcorr §6. z > 0: the first-named asset has run ahead. "Deviated" is read both ways in §9 — lagging (z ≤ −1) and leading (z ≥ +1) — because the brief did not say which, and the data decides (leading won).
7. "Pair trading strategy" = the classic rule (enter |z| ≥ 2, exit |z| ≤ 0.5 or 26 weeks, long cheap / short rich, 10 bp per leg per side), plus the long-only reading (the cheap leg's return vs the rich leg's). Evaluated as a rule, not run as a portfolio.
8. "Which asset should replace it" = twelve rules on the same SELL flips, each picking one candidate or falling back to cash; candidates are the other four assets; cash = `liquidEtfFinal.csv` (`Liquid`); the "correlated" threshold is a 52-week correlation ≥ 0.5 (`RP_CORR_MIN`).
9. The volatility aspect = 13-week realised vol by own signal state, around N100's flips, and correlation by vol regime (quintiles of N100's vol); the covariance split of §2 is the same question from the covariance side.

## Data issues
* Six sell-offs since 2016; Micro250 is in two. Small samples by nature — the study reports ranks and medians, not t-stats.
* Episode E1 (Jan 2016) begins at the first week of data: no supertrend state exists yet, so its signal row is blank.
* `india_vix.csv` ends 2025-09-15; it is only a context line on the timeline chart here.

* `liquidEtfFinal.csv` has DD-MM-YYYY dates and one value column; the loader handles both.
* Gold's own calendar (MCX) runs a few sessions past the NSE's last date; `current` tables use the last week every index has a close.
